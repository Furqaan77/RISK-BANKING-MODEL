# UAE retail credit benchmark: top retail banks and published secured-finance terms (as of 2026-11-09)

**How to read the tags.** Each cell carries a tag such as [E3/H].
- **Letter = which source family:**
  - E = Emirates NBD (ENBD) and Emirates Islamic (EI)
  - F = First Abu Dhabi Bank (FAB), Abu Dhabi Commercial Bank (ADCB) and Al Hilal Bank (AHB)
  - D = Dubai Islamic Bank (DIB) and Abu Dhabi Islamic Bank (ADIB)
  - M = Mashreq (MSQ), Mashreq Al Islami (MAI) and RAKBANK (RAK)
  - R = ranking and market sources
- **Confidence after the slash:**
  - H = read directly on the bank page, Key Facts Statement (KFS), schedule of charges or results filing.
  - M = bank content seen only in a search extract, or a figure read off a chart.
  - L = broker, aggregator or press.

**Other conventions.**
- "n/p" means not published.
- Fees shown "incl. VAT" are the 1% / AED 10k levels plus 5% VAT (1.05% / AED 10,500).
- STL means the customer transfers their salary to the bank.
- Most bank pages were read directly, by fetching the HTML and extracting the KFS PDFs. Exceptions are tagged M or L.

**Caveats on the ranking.**
- ADIB (#2) and FAB (#3) are effectively tied at FY25, about AED 94–95bn each. ADIB's figure is mostly UAE; FAB's includes international books. On UAE-only retail, and on the 2026 trajectory, ADIB is clearly second.
- By customers the order shifts: ADCB (2.2m UAE retail) is ahead of DIB (1.6–1.7m).
- A third-party study puts ENBD group at "nearly a third" of UAE deposits, loans and cards [R14/L].
- An analyst note gives ADIB retail as 37.9% of its book [R20/L]. This conflicts with the bank's presentation (about 51% of AED 185.6bn), so the bank's figure is used.

**Implications for an Islamic lender's policy.**
- **Structure.** Every Islamic peer uses IMBT for ready homes and Istisna'a + forward Ijarah for under-construction property. No published product uses Diminishing Musharaka; one aggregator claim that EI does conflicts with EI's own KFS.
- **Auto.** Auto finance is Murabaha everywhere; ADIB also uses Musawama.
- **Settlement and late payment.** Islamic peers apply a 1% early-settlement cap through rebate of unearned profit (Ibra). Late payment is handled as a charity undertaking of AED 100–735.

## (E) Notable 2025–2026 changes

1. **Rates turned up.**
   - The CBUAE Base Rate was 3.65% after the 2025 cuts and was raised to **3.90% effective 17-Sep-2026** [R16/L].
   - 3M EIBOR was 4.39% on 1-Oct-26 and 1M EIBOR 4.09% on 2-Nov-26 [F1/H], against about 3.5% in January [R16/L].
   - Banks repriced: ENBD 2-yr STL went from 3.89% to 4.19% (Oct-26) [E29/L]; RAKBANK's July promotion at 3.89% compares with 4.69% in the Oct-26 KFS [M42/L, M25/H]; Mashreq withdrew its premium tier [M21/L]. Press expects higher stress rates [R17/L].
   - Most published fixed teasers (3.85–4.69%) now sit **below** the variable all-in rate (EIBOR + 1.25–1.99%, about 5.4–6.4%). Reversion margins and floors therefore matter more than the headline rate.
2. **Prepayment liberalised.**
   - EI: 30% per year free from 1-Jul-26 [E44/H].
   - ADCB: 30% per year in the Feb-26 KFS [F6/H].
   - ADIB: 30% per year [D42/H].
   - FAB: 25% per year, variable period only [F27/H].
3. **Age and non-resident terms.** Mashreq raised the salaried maximum age at maturity from 65 to 70 and non-resident LTV from 60% to 65% in its KFS of 19-Jun-26 [M4 vs M6/H]. The CBUAE age cap has not existed since Oct-2019 [R15/H].
4. **Off-plan "35% complete / 50% paid" model is spreading** through developer tie-ups:
   - DAMAC–ADIB (Mar-25) [D57/M]
   - Sobha–ADIB (Jan-26) [D58/M] and Sobha–ENBD (Apr-26) [E27/H]
   - Dubai Holding RE–ENBD and –ADIB (2026) [E28/L]
   - DIB off-plan product and Expo City Dubai deal (Oct-26) [D4/H][D29/M]
5. **Housing programmes.**
   - Dubai First-Time Home Buyer programme launched 2-Jul-25 for properties under AED 5m [E71/M]. Mashreq's 2026 terms are the richest: −0.25% margin, 0% processing [M7/H].
   - ADHA top-up partnerships expanded (Aug-25) [E72/M]. FAB, ADCB and ADIB offer up to AED 500k with a 50% profit subsidy. ADIB launched instant digital ADHA pre-approval (25-Jun-26) [D53/H].
6. **Selective tightening.**
   - EI capped expat FTV at 70% for some sectors and suspended applicants from hospitality, travel and real-estate employers (Apr–Jun 26) [E59/L].
   - ENBD dropped its 5-yr fixed, reinstated processing fees and added a 1% fee on cash-out (Apr-26) [E31/L]. It also ended new-to-bank and rental-income pricing (Oct-26) [E29/L].
7. **Capital.** From Jan-2026 the CBUAE counter-cyclical buffer raised capital minima by 30bp [R2/H].
8. **Retail growth.**
   - ENBD retail loans +25% in 2025 [R1/H].
   - ADIB home finance +38% YoY to 3Q26 [R3/H].
   - DIB consumer origination +90% [R6/H].
9. **Documentation refresh.** Banks re-issued KFS documents across 2025–26:
   - ENBD home Feb-26
   - EI home Apr-26
   - DIB home May-26 and auto Sep-26
   - FAB Sep-26
   - RAK Oct-26
   - ADCB Feb-26
   - AHB Mar-26 and Sep-26
   - Mashreq car Dec-25
   Green mortgages are now standard: FAB, Mashreq, RAK (Barjeel), DIB Nest and ADCB.

## Gaps, conflicts and caveats

**Not published by any bank:**
- LTV above AED 5m (except RAK's 70%)
- LTV for second or investment homes (except Mashreq 60%)
- stress rates
- employment vintage (except ENBD; DIB auto 1 month; FAB Islamic / Mashreq auto 6 months)
- used-car age rules at FAB, ADIB, EI and Mashreq
- FAB's mortgage minimum salary and age limits

Where a bank publishes nothing, assume the CBUAE caps (75/70, 65/60, 50%) apply, but this is unverified.

**Internal conflicts to confirm with the banks:**
- **ADIB:** "85% for nationals and expats" conflicts with the 80% expat cap. Its pages also give different maximums for non-resident (5m vs 30m) and Finance Against Property (15m vs 30m).
- **ENBD:** maximum amount appears as 25m and as 15m; loan-against-property LTV for nationals as 70%, 75% and 85%; FD overdraft as 90% and 80%.
- **DIB:** early-settlement fee 1% + VAT (KFS) vs 1.05% (schedule of charges), which is the same economics.
- **FAB:** processing fee 1.05% (KFS) vs 0.525% (schedule of charges).
- **ADHA top-up minimum income:** 30k at FAB and ADIB vs 8k at ADCB.

**Stale sources.** Treat these rate ranges with care: ADCB auto KFS (Jan/Feb-25), ADIB auto KFS (Apr-25), the ENBD price guide (2021) and the EI broker grid (expired 31-Aug-26).

**CBUAE caps.** The early-settlement cap (1% / AED 10k) and the removal of the age limit were verified against the CBUAE Oct-2019 release [R15/H], and the 50% DBR via ENBD's help page [E14/H]. The LTV caps (85/80, 75/70, 65/60, off-plan 50%) and the 20% / 60-month auto limits are taken from the brief; they match the bank documents but I did not check them against CBUAE text.

**FAB ranking figure.** FAB's personal/retail loans (~AED 94bn) were read from chart labels in the presentation (tagged M). They are consistent with mortgages at 7% plus personal loans and cards at 7% of the AED 679bn book.
