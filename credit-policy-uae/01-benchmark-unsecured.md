## A4. Special segments
- **Salary advance:**
  - DIB FlexiSalary: min salary AED 2,000, up to 13k, fixed profit 80–200, no fee.
  - ADIB Yusr: STL customers; min 2,500 (listed employer) / 5,000 (other); up to 50% of net salary or 50k; max 51 days; 0.99–3.99% per month flat.
- **Below AED 5,000:** DIB and RAKBANK nationals start at AED 3,000.
- **New-to-country:** FAB (10k salary, 600k, from 12.75%); Mashreq "New to UAE / New to employer"; ENBD (no minimum service); EI (1 month).
- **Young nationals:** RAKBANK from 18y 6m with a guarantor.
- **Retirees:** DBR 30% of pension.
- **First-instalment grace for nationals:** FAB 365 days, DIB 240, ADIB 210, RAKBANK 180, ENBD / Mashreq 120.
- **Legacy National Loans Scheme (NLS):** 3M EIBOR, up to 300 months.

## D. 2025–2026 changes
1. **The CBUAE scrapped the AED 5,000 minimum salary (Nov 2025).**
   - Most banks still sit at ≥ 5,000.
   - Below 5,000: DIB 3,000; RAKBANK nationals 3,000; DIB FlexiSalary 2,000; ADIB Yusr 2,500.
   - Unchanged: 20× salary, 50% DBR, 48 months; repayments run through WPS.
2. **No CBUAE rate cap.** Pricing runs up to 31.99%. The fee caps produce the uniform 1.05% / 10,500 / 2,625.
3. **Salary-transfer-free and score-based products are growing:** ENBD high-AECB waiver; Mashreq NEO; RAKBANK non-STL with AECB floors; FAB Islamic non-STL.
4. **Pricing changes:** EIBOR-linked variable personal loans (ENBD); first-time salary advance at 0.99% (ADIB Yusr).
5. **Caveats:**
   - Card limit multiples, most AECB floors and max ages are rarely published.
   - Mashreq Al Islami has no public PF terms.
   - ADCB figures are M confidence.
   - ADIB's rate basis needs confirmation.
