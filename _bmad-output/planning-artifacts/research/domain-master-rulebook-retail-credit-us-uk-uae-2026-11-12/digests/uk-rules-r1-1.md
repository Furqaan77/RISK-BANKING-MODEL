## Part C: leads and contradictions

C1. **Credit-limit increases: voluntary or rule?** PS18/4 (2018) describes limit-increase controls as voluntary industry remedies (B21), but the current CONC (July 2026) contains hard rules CONC 6.7.7R-6.7.9R (B29). Treat B29 as the current position; B21 is superseded.

C2. **Stress test "flexibility" vs hard floor.** The FCA page (B6) stresses that firms can design their own stress rate, but current MCOB 11.6.18R(3) still requires a minimum +1 percentage point rise over 5 years (B43). Both are true: method is flexible, the +1pp floor is not. MCOB 11.6.18R(2)(b) still refers to "any prevailing FPC recommendation", but none prevails since August 2022.

C3. **LTI limit wording.** Many market sources still describe "lenders can only do 15% at 4.5x+". From 28 Oct 2026 that is WRONG at firm level: the 15% applies to the market aggregate only (B9-B10). A lender's own policy can exceed 15% unless the PRA announces an adjustment.

C4. **Representative APR 51% under review.** CP26/15 (closed 17 June 2026) floats 66% and asks whether the mandatory representative example should go. Final policy statement not found. Re-check in 2027.

C5. **CCA reform timing.** HMT May 2026 policy statement + Financial Services and Markets Bill will move information requirements (SECCI, statements, arrears notices) into FCA rules. ss.56/75/75A/140A-C deferred. Commencement unknown. Our rules must be configurable because the source of the rule (statute vs FCA rule) will change.

C6. **FCA Mortgage Rule Review CP26/18** (June 2026, first-time buyers, variable income, older borrowers, past credit problems): outcome not found. May change MCOB 11.6 affordability for these groups.

C7. **Equality Act pending changes.** legislation.gov.uk shows outstanding changes to the Equality Act inserted by an "Equality Act 2025 (2025 c.36) s.34" against Sch.3 para 20A's page. Could not confirm what this Act is; verify before relying on the age exception text.

C8. **Data automated decisions guidance.** DUAA s.80 is in force (5 Feb 2026); ICO guidance on automated decision-making and possible Secretary of State regulations under Art 22D (defining "meaningful human involvement") are pending. For our "grade ≥ 7 goes to a human" design, a Refer with real human review likely takes those cases out of "solely automated"; declines at grades 9-10 made by the model alone ARE solely automated significant decisions and need Art 22C safeguards.

C9. **Scope of PRA SS1/23.** Formally only IRB/IMA/IMM firms, but the PRA expects all firms to manage model risk; non-IRB banks commonly adopt it voluntarily. Treat as best practice for our on-prem model.

C10. **Basel 3.1 go-live** for residential mortgage risk weights: near-final PS9/24; final implementation date (expected 1 January 2027) not verified.

C11. **HCSTC price cap review** stopped at stage 1 (Sept 2026); cap unchanged but the FCA will keep monitoring.
