## Reading notes for the rulebook (plain language)

- **Who is most "open":** NatWest has the clearest published grid and the highest LTI (6.5x for joint income >=GBP150k at <=75% LTV). Barclays allows 6x for household income >=GBP75k at <=85% LTV. Lloyds and HSBC cap first-time buyers at 5.5x (Lloyds from GBP40k household income; HSBC from GBP35k sole / GBP55k joint), both at <=90% LTV.
- **Who is most "careful" on age:** NatWest stops at 75 (repayment) / 70 (interest-only). HSBC, Barclays and Lloyds allow up to 80 (verify for HSBC and Barclays).
- **High-LTV tightening is common:** NatWest drops LTI to 4.0-5.0x at 95% LTV; Lloyds and HSBC enhanced multiples stop at 90% LTV; Barclays 6x stops at 85% LTV. This is a "tighten as risk rises" pattern our rule layers can copy.
- **Unsecured pricing band:** headline personal-loan APRs sit at 6.8%-7.9%; mainstream card APRs at 24.9%-25.9%; card late fee GBP12 everywhere; arranged overdraft 29.9%-39.9% EAR.
- **Book quality is strong:** mortgage Stage 3 around 1.2% (LBG); book average LTV 45%-55%; Barclays 90-day mortgage arrears 0.1%. Unsecured Stage 3 is a bit higher (LBG cards 1.5%, loans/overdrafts 1.6%).
