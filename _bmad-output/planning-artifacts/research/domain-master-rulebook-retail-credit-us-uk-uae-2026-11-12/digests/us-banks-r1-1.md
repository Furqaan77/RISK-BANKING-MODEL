## Bank 1: JPMorgan Chase (Chase)

### 1. Personal loan
- **Not offered.** Chase has no unsecured personal loan for new or existing customers. Aggregators (Fortune, LendingTree, Credible) all say so. [L for the "no product" claim from aggregators; Chase product menus list cards, mortgage, auto, business, no personal loan.]
- The nearest product is **My Chase Loan**: selected existing cardholders can borrow against unused card credit and repay in fixed instalments. It is not an open application product. Eligibility rules are not published by Chase (one aggregator says card open 180+ days; unconfirmed, L).

### 2. Credit card: Chase Freedom Unlimited (no annual fee, mainstream)
- Purchase APR after intro: **18.24%–27.74% variable** on the pricing-and-terms page dated 12/16/2025 (Prime 6.75%). The live product page (fetched 2026-11-12) shows **18.49%–27.99%**, built as Prime 7.00% + 11.49% / + 20.99%, capped at 29.99%. Use the live page figure; see Part C.
- Intro: 0% for 15 months on purchases and balance transfers.
- Late fee: **up to $40**. Returned payment: up to $40.
- Penalty APR: **up to 29.99% variable**; triggered by one missed minimum payment or a returned payment; "could potentially remain in effect indefinitely."
- Annual fee: **$0**.
- Balance transfer fee: greater of $5 or 3% in the first 60 days, then greater of $5 or 5%.
- Credit / income requirement: **none published as a number.** Chase says it reviews the credit report and the information given, and may use employment and income. One-card-per-rewards-programme rule applies.

### 3. Mortgage (Chase own page)
- Conventional (conforming): down payment **3%–20%** of price. Terms named: 30-yr fixed, 15-yr fixed, 7/6 ARM.
- **DreaMaker** (affordable): as low as **3% down**, 30-yr fixed, income limits apply (no number on the page). Chase newsroom: income limit removed in communities where its $5,000/$7,500 Homebuyer Grant applies. Minimum score 620 is reported by CNBC / The Mortgage Reports only (L).
- FHA 3.5% down (15/20/25/30-yr); VA 0% down (10–30-yr).
- **Jumbo: minimum 15% down; maximum loan $9.5 million.**
- Max DTI: **not published.** Minimum credit score: not published as a number.
- Advertised 30-yr fixed (conventional purchase): **6.875% rate / 7.078% APR**, "as of 10:57 AM ET, November 10th, 2026", for a $350,000 loan, ZIP 60629, with **2.029 discount points**. LTV and score assumptions not stated.

### 4. Auto loan
- **Offered**, but only for cars bought from a **Chase network dealer** (online pre-approval or at the dealer). No private-party purchases.
- Vehicle limits: **max 10 years old, under 120,000 miles**, personal use only, some exotic makes excluded. No down payment required. No Chase account needed.
- Min/max amount: not published. Max term: not published on Chase's page (NerdWallet says 12–84 months, L). APR from: not published.
- Relationship discount: 0.25% rate cut for clients with average $150,000+ balances.

### 5. Risk appetite signals (3Q26 earnings release and financial supplement, quarter to 30 Sep 2026)
- Card Services **net charge-off rate 2.91%** (2Q26 3.34%; 3Q25 3.15%). Full-year 2026 guide about **3.2%**.
- Card Services **30+ days past due 1.97%**, **90+ days past due 0.96%** (3Q25: 2.14% / 1.07%). [M: read from a text-extracted table where labels were offset one row; values match the earnings-search summary.]
- Auto: NCO 0.41%, 30+ DPD 0.96%. Home Lending 30+ DPD 0.79%.
- Book size (period-end 30 Sep 2026): credit card loans **$252.7bn**; consumer excluding card **$393.1bn**; **total consumer loans $645.8bn** (firm total loans $1,584.6bn). CCB segment loans $593.7bn; Auto $73.0bn.
- Management said on the 3Q26 call it is being more selective in card underwriting (secondary source summary, L).

### 6. Age / residency / ITIN
- **Not published** by Chase on any page fetched. Aggregators and forums conflict on whether Chase accepts ITIN applications for cards (Part C). Auto: Chase account not required; no age or residency rule stated.

## Bank 2: Bank of America (BofA)

### 1. Personal loan
- **Not offered.** No unsecured personal loan on BofA's site. Aggregators (Credible, Bankrate, Rate Chaser) agree; Rate Chaser says BofA pulled back from unsecured personal lending after 2008. [L]
- Nearest product: **Balance Assist**, a small short-term loan for existing checking customers only. BofA's page could not be fetched (HTTP 405), so amount, fee and eligibility are **not captured** this round (Part D).

### 2. Credit card: Bank of America Unlimited Cash Rewards (no annual fee, mainstream)
- Purchase APR after intro: **17.74%–27.74% variable** (Prime as of 09/30/2026). Same range on the live product page.
- Intro: 0% for 15 billing cycles on purchases and on balance transfers made in the first 60 days.
- Late fee: **up to $41** (pricing table). Other BofA disclosures seen in search show the structure "$30 first time, $41 if late again within 6 billing cycles; no fee if balance is $100 or less" [M, seen in search snippet of another BofA card disclosure].
- Penalty APR: **up to 29.99% variable**, triggered by a late payment, applies to **new transactions** only, and "will apply indefinitely". Agreement text: "The calculated Penalty APR using this variable rate formula will not exceed 29.99%."
- Annual fee: **$0**. Balance transfer fee 3% for first 60 days, then 5%. Cash advance fees 5%; foreign transaction 3%.
- Credit / income requirement: **not published.**

### 3. Mortgage (BofA own pages)
- **Affordable Loan Solution**: **3% down**; one-unit **max 97% LTV / 105% CLTV** (CLTV above 95% only if the second lien is an affordable down-payment-assistance programme). Income and loan-amount limits apply and vary by location (no number published). Mortgage insurance required. Homebuyer education may be required for first-time buyers.
- **Jumbo**: minimum **5% of purchase price** (page wording "5% or more of purchase price"; ~95% LTV inferred, not stated). Terms 30-yr fixed, 15-yr fixed, 5y/6m ARM (disclosures also name 7y/6m, 10y/6m). Jumbo threshold stated as $832,750 single-family ($1,249,125 high-cost areas). "Stricter underwriting rules", no numbers.
- FHA / VA: offered; no down-payment number on the page fetched.
- Max DTI: **not published.** Minimum credit score: **not published** (rate assumptions use 740+).
- Advertised 30-yr fixed: **not published** at fetch time. The rate page showed template placeholders ("Rate X.XXX%") with assumptions: $200,000 loan, ZIP 95464, 5%+ down, credit score 740+.

### 4. Auto loan
- **Offered** (direct; new and used from dealers; refinance also exists but not on the page fetched).
- APR from: **5.24% new / 5.44% used**, fixed, 60 months, "as of October 19, 2026", assumes excellent credit. Relationship (BofA Rewards) discounts 0.10%–0.50%, not included in the advertised rate.
- Online terms: **48, 60 or 72 months** (others via loan officer). Prequalification shows amounts **$10,000–$85,000**.
- Minimum amount **$7,500 ($8,000 in Minnesota)**, calculator ceiling $1,000,000; vehicle exclusions: older than **10 calendar years**, **125,000+ miles**, value under $6,000 [M: from BofA auto FAQ / calculator, seen via search snippet; search tool flagged the FAQ as possibly old].

### 5. Risk appetite signals (3Q26 earnings presentation, 14 Oct 2026; 3Q26 10-Q)
- Credit card **net charge-off rate 3.22%** in 3Q26 (2Q26 3.55%; 3Q25 3.46%). "Both early- and late-stage delinquencies improved vs. 3Q25."
- Consumer NCO ratio 0.81% (consumer NCOs $997MM). Total company NCO ratio 0.44%.
- Consumer **30+ days performing past due $4,584MM** (of which $415MM fully insured). On $488bn consumer loans this is **about 0.94%** (derived by us). Consumer 90+ performing past due $1,431MM (~0.29%, derived).
- Credit card 30+ DPD about **$2.5bn** and 90+ about **$1.2bn** at 30 Sep 2026 (10-Q text via search snippet, M). On ~$106bn card loans this is **~2.4% (30+)** and **~1.1% (90+)** (derived, M).
- Book size (3Q26, average): **consumer loans $488bn** = home lending $264bn + consumer credit card $106bn + direct/indirect and other $118bn. Total loans $1,251bn period-end.

### 6. Age / residency / ITIN
- **Not published** on any BofA page fetched. Card agreement includes a Military Lending Act disclosure (standard). Aggregators list BofA among card issuers that accept ITIN applications (Bankrate/Finder, L), not verified on a BofA page.

## Bank 3: Wells Fargo (WF)

### 1. Personal loan
- **Offered, existing customers only.** "We only offer personal loans to existing Wells Fargo customers"; a qualifying consumer product must be open **at least 12 months**.
- Amount **$3,000–$100,000**. Terms **12–84 months** (12/24/36/48/60/72/84).
- APR **6.74%–25.74%**, rates "as of 10/07/2026". The lowest rate includes a **0.25% relationship discount** (needs WF consumer checking + autopay from a WF deposit account) and "assumes excellent credit history".
- Fees: **no origination fee**, no closing fee, no prepayment penalty.
- Minimum credit score / income: **not published.**
- Application asks for: **SSN or ITIN**, citizenship status, date of birth, permanent physical address, gross monthly income and sources, employment, housing payment. Minimum age and residency rule: not stated.

### 2. Credit card: Wells Fargo Active Cash (no annual fee, mainstream)
- Purchase APR after intro: **18.74%, 24.74% or 28.74% variable** (three tiers, not a range), margins 11.74 / 17.74 / 21.74 over Prime **7.00% as of 11/12/2026**. Maximum APR 29.99%.
- Intro: 0% for 12 months on purchases and qualifying balance transfers.
- Late fee: **up to $40**.
- Penalty APR: **none stated** in the Active Cash terms (the terms page does not list a penalty APR).
- Annual fee **$0**. Balance transfer: greater of $5 or 3% for 120 days, then up to 5% (min $5).
- Minimum credit limit **$1,000**. No income or score minimum published; WF may "investigate your credit, employment, and income history". Rules: no intro offer if you opened an Active Cash in the last 48 months; may not qualify for another WF consumer card opened within the last 4 months.
- Note: a search snippet of the same page showed older tiers 19.24% / 24.24% / 29.24% (Part C).

### 3. Mortgage (WF own pages)
- Conventional fixed: **as low as 3% down** (mortgage insurance required). Terms **10 to 40 years** (30/20/15 highlighted).
- **Dream. Plan. Home.**: 3% down for borrowers at or below **80% of area median income** with limited credit history; lower rate. [M: WF page seen via search snippet only.]
- **Jumbo: as little as 10.01% down, no mortgage insurance**, primary residence, **max loan $2,000,000** in the 10.01%–19.99% down tier.
- DTI / credit score: education page only says "Many lenders prefer DTIs below 36%" and "Conventional mortgages often require a credit score of at least 620". These are general education text, **not a WF policy limit** (M). WF-specific max DTI: **not published.**
- Advertised 30-yr fixed: **not published** on the WF rates page at fetch time (rates only via a personal quote tool). An aggregator reported ~6.375% / 6.529% APR in early May 2026 (L, stale; not used in Part A).

### 4. Auto loan
- **Indirect only.** "Wells Fargo auto loans are only available through our nationwide network of nearly 11,000 dealerships." No direct application; refinance not mentioned.
- Amount limits, max term, vehicle age, APR: **not published** on the page fetched.
- Activity: auto originations $10.1bn in 3Q26 (+15% YoY).

### 5. Risk appetite signals (3Q26 earnings supplement and presentation, Oct 2026)
- Credit card **net charge-off rate 3.73%** in 3Q26 ($554MM); 2Q26 was 4.18%.
- Credit card **30+ days delinquency 2.69%**, **90+ days 1.34%** (period-end 30 Sep 2026). Home lending 30+ DPD 0.33%.
- Consumer NCO 0.70% of average consumer loans (3Q25: 0.73%). Auto NCO 0.57%. Total company NCO 0.38%.
- Book size (period-end 30 Sep 2026): **total consumer loans $401.0bn** = residential mortgage $240.4bn + credit card $59.5bn + auto $60.2bn + other consumer $40.9bn. Total loans $1,048.1bn.
- Growth signal: ~661k new card accounts in 3Q26; average card loans up vs. 3Q25.

### 6. Age / residency / ITIN
- **ITIN accepted** on the personal loan application ("Social Security Number or Individual Tax Identification Number (ITIN)"); citizenship status is asked. No minimum age or residency rule published. Card terms: identity by name, address, date of birth; no ITIN statement.

## Bank 4: Citi (Citibank, N.A.)

### 1. Personal loan
- **Offered to new and existing customers** "who meet credit and income requirements".
- Amount **$2,000–$30,000**; existing customers with an eligible Citi deposit account or Citi card may get **up to $50,000**.
- APR **10.24%–18.49%** fixed for the loan term, rates "as of 9-29-2026". **0.25% autopay discount**; relationship-tier discounts (amount not stated); only the highest discount applies.
- Terms **12–60 months**. Fees: "No Origination Fees. No fees whatsoever."
- Eligibility: **age 18+ (21+ in Puerto Rico)**; **no co-applicants**; existing Citi card customers who have been customers **under 12 months and have no Citi deposit account are not eligible**.
- Minimum credit score / income: **not published** (learning centre: "established credit and income history").
- Residency / citizenship / ITIN: **not published** (learning centre says the application "typically asks for" SSN).

### 2. Credit card: Citi Double Cash (no annual fee, mainstream)
- Purchase APR: **17.49%–27.49% variable** on Citi's pricing fact sheet (= Prime + 10.74% to 20.74%; file last modified Nov 2025, so Prime 6.75% era). Citi's live product page showed placeholders on fetch; a search snippet of the same page showed **18.49%–28.74%** (Part C). At Prime 7.00% (rate on 11/12/2026 per WF terms) the fact-sheet margins give 17.74%–27.74% (our calculation).
- Late fee: **up to $41** (agreement: $30, or $41 if another late payment within the next 6 billing periods).
- Penalty APR: **up to 29.99% variable** (Prime + up to 26.74%, capped 29.99%); triggered by a late payment or a returned payment; "may apply indefinitely".
- Annual fee **none**. Balance transfer: greater of $5 or 3% within 4 months, then greater of $5 or 5%. Cash advance APR 29.74%.
- Minimum income / credit: **not published** ("subject to credit approval").

### 3. Mortgage (now marketed as "Mortgage.com by Citi"; citi.com mortgage pages redirect to mortgage.com, Citibank N.A. NMLS #412915)
- **HomeRun** (affordable, Citi-identified markets only, primary residence, purchase or refinance): **3% down** single-family/condo/co-op on loans up to **$832,750** (up to $1,249,125 in some high-cost areas; some high-cost markets need 5%). Borrower's own funds can be **as little as 1%** (rest from gifts / assistance). Two-unit: 5% own funds. **No mortgage insurance**. Income **no more than 20% above area median income** (or property in a Citi-identified region). **DTI up to 43%**. Credit: "typically requires higher credit scores than FHA" (no number).
- VA: no down payment. FHA: offered (no % on page). Conventional and jumbo: offered, **minimum down payment not published**; jumbo page not found (404).
- Terms (years): **not published** on pages fetched (fixed and ARM offered).
- Advertised 30-yr fixed: **not published** (placeholder "X.XXX%" on mortgage.com at fetch time).

### 4. Auto loan
- **Not offered** in the US. Citi's own learning centre points car buyers to its personal loan; aggregators say Citi exited auto lending (CitiFinancial Auto, around 2010–2013; exact year conflicts, L).

### 5. Risk appetite signals (3Q26 earnings presentation, 13 Oct 2026)
- U.S. Consumer Cards (USCC) **NCL rate 3.66%**, **90+ DPD 1.30%**; EOP card loans **$178bn** (3Q25 $168bn). Full-year 2026 U.S. cards NCL guide **~4.0%** (low end of 4.0–4.5%).
- By product: **General Purpose Credit Cards (incl. Double Cash) NCL 3.51%, 90+ DPD 1.21%**, EOP loans $150.3bn. Private Label NCL 4.19%, 90+ DPD 1.92%, EOP $27.6bn.
- Total EOP consumer loans **$418bn** (includes Wealth and Legacy Franchises, e.g. Mexico consumer; not US-only).
- 30+ DPD for cards: **not published** in the presentation (Citi reports 90+).
- Note: Citi's 3Q26 10-Q describes a re-segmented "USCC = GPCC + PLCC" view; the old "Branded Cards / Retail Services" labels are no longer used.

### 6. Age / residency / ITIN
- Personal loan: **18+ (21+ Puerto Rico)**. Residency, citizenship and ITIN policy: **not published.** Deposit-account agreement (not loans) allows foreign passport ID where no SSN/TIN exists.

# Part C: Data conflicts

1. **Chase Freedom Unlimited APR.** Live product page: 18.49%-27.99% (Prime 7.00%). Pricing-and-terms page dated 12/16/2025: 18.24%-27.74% (Prime 6.75%). Same margins (11.49 / 20.99), different Prime. Use the live page. Aggregators also show 18.99%-28.49% and 20.49%-29.24% (older); discard.
2. **Prime Rate dates.** WF terms say Prime 7.00% as of 11/12/2026; Chase live page also builds on 7.00%; Chase PDF (Dec 2025) and Citi fact sheet imply 6.75%. BofA uses Prime "as of 09/30/2026" but does not print the figure. All card APRs float, so they move together.
3. **Citi Double Cash APR.** Fact sheet 17.49%-27.49% vs. product page snippet 18.49%-28.74%. The product-page figures sit about 1.0-1.25 points higher, which may mean new margins, not only a Prime change. Not confirmed: the live page served placeholders. Part A uses the fact sheet, confidence M.
4. **WF Active Cash APR tiers.** Terms page fetched today: 18.74 / 24.74 / 28.74%. A search snippet of the same URL: 19.24 / 24.24 / 29.24% (older version). Use today's fetch.
5. **BofA late fee wording.** UCR pricing table: "Up to $41". Other BofA disclosures: $30 first, $41 repeat within 6 cycles, none if balance is $100 or less. Not a real conflict (cap vs. schedule). A search summary also mentioned a "1% of new balance, $35 minimum" clause; not seen in our own text extract. Treat as unverified.
6. **BofA penalty APR.** A search summary said one agreement gives a margin up to 26.74% "currently 33.74%". Our own extract shows a hard cap: "will not exceed 29.99%". Use 29.99%.
7. **Chase auto term.** No Chase page states a max term. NerdWallet: 12-84 months (used); Finder: shortest 48 months; another: up to 7 years. Not used in Part A.
8. **BofA auto mileage / new-car definition.** FAQ (via snippet) says 125,000+ miles excluded; new-car rule conflicts (under 6,000 vs. under 10,000 miles). FAQ may be old. Chase uses 120,000 miles.
9. **Delinquency metric not like-for-like** (card 30+ for Chase and WF; all-consumer 30+ performing for BofA; card 90+ only for Citi). Do not rank banks on `consumer_30dpd_pct` without this note. Card 30+ like-for-like where available: Chase 1.97%, WF 2.69%, BofA about 2.4% (derived).
10. **Book size basis differs.** BofA = quarterly average; others period-end. Citi $418bn includes Mexico consumer (Legacy Franchises) and Wealth.
11. **Card NCO scope differs.** Chase Card Services includes co-brand; Citi GPCC excludes private label (USCC incl. PLCC = 3.66%); WF and BofA = whole card book.
12. **Citi auto exit year.** Aggregators say 2010 or 2013. Not material.
