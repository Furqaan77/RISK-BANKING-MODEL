# US banks: small-business and commercial / corporate lending disclosures (round 1, digest 1)

- Scope: JPMorgan Chase (JPM), Bank of America (BAC), Wells Fargo (WFC), Citi (C)
- Accessed: 2026-11-13. Period: 3Q26 (30 Sep 2026) unless noted.
- Rule: only what the banks (or named third parties) publish. Banks do not publish internal credit policies. Gaps are written "not published". Nothing filled from memory.
- Plain-language notes: "NCO" = net charge-off (loans written off, less recoveries), as a % of average loans, annualised. "Nonaccrual" = loans where the bank has stopped booking interest because repayment is in doubt. "Criticized" = loans rated special mention, substandard or doubtful under US regulators' definitions (an early-warning bucket). "Wholesale" = JPM's word for business, corporate and institutional lending.

## Part C: conflicts

- JPM line of credit maximum: the Chase product page says $10,000 to $500,000 for existing Chase for Business customers. A search-engine summary of chase.com said online requests are $10,000 to $250,000, with larger amounts in branch. Both may be true (online vs. banker channel). Use the product page; mark "verify".
- JPM line of credit collateral: the Chase page says "Chase only offers secured lines of credit". Third parties disagree (Finder says secured; SuperMoney says unsecured only). The bank page wins.
- JPM time in business: the bank page only says ownership/management unchanged for two years. NerdWallet quotes "24 months" in business. Treat 2 years as M confidence.
- BAC office criticized: the 10-Q says office reservable criticized fell 26% in nine months (31 Dec 2025 to 30 Sep 2026). Management on the call said "down 50% or 60%" over a year. Different time windows and a verbal estimate. Use the 10-Q figure.
- BAC "small business" has two meanings: (a) "US small business commercial" $23.9bn, which is about half small business credit cards; (b) the "Business Banking" segment with $13.3bn loans. They overlap only partly. Do not add them together.
- WFC time in business: the BusinessLine product page targets "two or more years in business"; another Wells Fargo page says six months or more. Unresolved; mark "verify".
- WFC annual fee: one WF page says waived in year 1; Bankrate says there is an annual fee. Amount not confirmed.
- WFC CRE share: bank table says 13% of total loans; simple division gives 12.6% (132.0 / 1,048.1). The difference is rounding. Use the bank's 13%.
- Citi CRE has two measures: (a) by industry, commercial real estate exposure $81.0bn total / $52.0bn funded; (b) by loan type, "mortgage and real estate" corporate loans $30.1bn ($19.5bn North America + $10.6bn outside). The industry view counts loans to real-estate firms of any type. The CSV uses (a) funded. Do not compare directly with BAC/WFC "CRE loans", which are loan-type measures.
- Comparability: "criticized" is measured differently. JPM = wholesale exposure incl. commitments (excl. HFS/fair value); BAC = reservable utilized exposure; WFC = loans only (sum of parts); Citi = total exposure incl. unfunded. Do not rank the four banks on criticized $ without this note.
- SBA rankings: M&T says it ranked "fifth nationally" ($326.4m) while U.S. Bank says No. 4 with $943m, and Coleman's six-month list had Northeast Bank fifth. Rankings likely use different measures (count vs. dollars, part vs. full year). Low confidence.
