# US non-bank consumer lenders ("finance houses"): rules + 4 lender benchmarks

Digest id: us-fh-r1-1 · Research date: 2026-11-13 · Status: COMPLETE (2026-11-13)

Plain-language note for new risk staff: in the US a "finance company" is a lender that is **not a bank**. It has no federal charter. Each state where it lends gives it a licence and sets its price caps. Federal consumer laws (TILA, ECOA, FCRA, FDCPA, UDAAP) still apply to it. Values marked "verify" were not confirmed from a primary source on 2026-11-13.
