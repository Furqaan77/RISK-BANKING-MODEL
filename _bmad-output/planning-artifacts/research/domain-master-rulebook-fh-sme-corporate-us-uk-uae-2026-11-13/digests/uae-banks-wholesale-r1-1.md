# Digest: UAE banks wholesale / SME lending disclosures (r1-1)

- Scope: Emirates NBD, First Abu Dhabi Bank (FAB), ADCB, Mashreq. What each bank publishes about corporate (wholesale) and SME lending.
- Accessed: 2026-11-13. Sources: the banks' own results decks, press releases, annual reports, Pillar 3 reports and product pages.
- Rule: banks do not publish internal corporate credit policies. Only disclosed values are shown. A gap says "not published" or "not found". "Derived" means we did the arithmetic on the bank's own published figures.
- Watch the definitions. "Corporate" is not the same thing at each bank. ENBD's "C&IB" includes most sovereign lending. FAB's "IB" is a wholesale division. Some banks put SME inside "Retail" or "Business Banking". Do not compare the shares as if they mean the same thing.

## Part C: conflicts

- ENBD, FY25 gross loans: the deck says AED 658bn, but the FS sector note totals AED 665.4bn. The FS total is before deferred income of AED 1.45bn and probably uses a different perimeter or classification (for example Islamic receivables). Use the deck figure for ratios and the FS figure for sector shares.
- ENBD, real estate and construction share: 11% in the deck at H1-26 and 10.6% derived from the FS at FY25. The two figures are for different dates. No real conflict.
- ENBD, POS loan maximum: the campaign page says AED 5m and the fixed-rate KFS says AED 2m. Treat the KFS as binding for the fixed-rate variant. A variable-rate variant may have its own KFS (not checked).
- ENBD, NPL baseline: the press says "2.0% from 2.5% a year ago". The deck says 2.4% at FY25 and 2.5% at Q3-25. Both are consistent with the deck.
- FAB, Q3-26 loan size: the call transcript gives gross loans of AED 697bn and Gulf News gives about AED 679bn. Probably gross versus net. Use 697 for gross.
- FAB, Stage 3 versus NPL ratio: the FY25 Stage 3 share derived from the FS is 2.7% (3.3% with POCI), but the reported NPL ratio is about 2.1-2.2%. The FS gross figures include AED 7.0bn of suspended interest, and the bank's NPL definition differs. Do not swap one for the other.
- FAB, coverage: 108% (H1-26) and 114% (Q3-26) exclude collateral. The "over 160%/167%" figures include collateral. FAB coverage is not comparable with ENBD's 152%, which also excludes collateral.
- ADCB, FY25 gross loans: the FS sector note gives AED 413,901m, while the Q3-26 FS stage table gives AED 410,965m for Dec-25 (the gap is probably fair-value adjustments or POCI presentation). Sector shares use the sector note.
- ADCB, corporate share: 75.5% (CIBG share of net loans) versus 71% ("Corporate loans" as a product share of gross loans). These are different cuts. From 2026, CIBG no longer includes business banking.
- ADCB, SME turnover band: one ADCB page describes the retail business loan for owners with turnover "up to AED 5M" (search snippet), and the product page says "minimum turnover of AED 500,000". These are consistent if the product serves the AED 0.5m-5m band (inference, not stated).
- ADCB, coverage: the Sep-25 coverage of 187.3% fell to 156.0% at Sep-26 as the legacy corporate accounts were written off or resolved. Not a conflict, but do not quote the 187% figure as current.
- Mashreq, merchant loan maximum: the NeoBiz FAQ says "Merchant Lending Product: AED 5 Million", while the merchant tile on the same page says "Limit up to AED 4 million" (the page title also says "Get up to AED 4M"). Treat AED 4m as the current digital offer and AED 5m as the product cap (unconfirmed).
- Mashreq, construction share: the agency quote gives 9% (Mar-26) and the FS-derived figure is 10.2% (Jun-26, conventional + Islamic gross). Different dates and bases.
- Mashreq, NPL versus Stage 3: NPL ratio 0.9% versus Stage 3 1.2% at Jun-26. The definitions differ (for example, the NPL figure may be net of interest in suspense or follow a different denominator).
- Cross-bank: Mashreq and ADCB show NEGATIVE or very low cost of risk in 2026 because of recoveries. Do not use these as through-the-cycle loss rates.
