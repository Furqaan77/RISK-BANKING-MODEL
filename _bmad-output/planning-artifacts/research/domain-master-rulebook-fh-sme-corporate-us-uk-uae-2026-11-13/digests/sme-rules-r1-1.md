# Digest: SME lending rulebook (UAE, UK, US), round 1, file 1

- Topic: regulatory rules for bank lending to SMEs (small and medium enterprises) in the UAE, UK and US
- Accessed: 2026-11-13 (all sources)
- Method: web search and fetch, primary sources first. Nothing here is from memory. Items with no source found are in Part D.
- Status: complete for round 1. Written country by country, so Part A (one table schema) and Part B are split into UAE, UK and US blocks with the same columns. Parts C and D are at the end and cover all three countries.
- Param ID prefixes: SME = definition and conduct rules, GTE = guarantee schemes, CAP = capital, COL = collateral and guarantees, CHQ = cheques, BKR = bankruptcy.

## Part C: conflicts and leads

### Conflicts
1. **UAE in-force date of C 2/2026.** CMS and Pinsent Masons say "six months after Official Gazette publication" (Pinsent corrected an earlier "August" to September). One summary gives 13 Sep 2026. The rulebook blocked fetching (HTTP 403). Treat as "in force by Sept 2026; verify exact date".
2. **UAE SME definition age.** The only definition found is Cabinet Resolution 22/2016 (via CBUAE 2021 regulation). The resolution says it should be reviewed every three years. No newer national definition was found, but one may exist. The definition text inside C 2/2026 was not retrieved.
3. **UAE security cheques vs "adequate guarantees".** Abu Dhabi courts (2023) refused to enforce a security cheque taken on top of a mortgage (over-security). Court of Cassation 819/2025 said one cheque for the full facility can be "sufficient". A 2026 ruling dismissed a bank claim for missing post-dated cheques. Also, an English High Court view said old Art. 121 bis did not cover corporate borrowing. The 2025 law (Art. 150) names natural persons and **sole proprietorships**, which matters for micro-SMEs.
4. **UAE Decree-Law 6/2025 issue date.** Chambers: 8 Sep 2025; Global Law Experts: 10 Oct 2025.
5. **UAE capital.** Rulebook snippet gives AED 4m SME retail cap; the older 2009 Basel II guidance gave AED 10m. Use the current standards (effective Dec 2022).
6. **UK GGS turnover cap.** gov.uk scheme page (29 Jun 2026) says £45m; HMT news (13 Jul 2026) raises it to £54m with go-live "in coming weeks". The cap now matches the new medium-company turnover limit (£54m).
7. **UK GGS end date.** No official end date found; one trade source says "until 2030", an older one said March 2026.
8. **US Section 1071.** Old tiered dates (Jul 2026 / Jan 2027 / Oct 2027 after 2025 extensions) are **superseded** by the 2026 rule's single date of 1 Jan 2028. One source mentions a grace period to 31 Dec 2028 and first filing 1 Jun 2029; not verified.
9. **US NY disclosure threshold.** Most sources say NY CFDL covers deals up to $2.5m; one 2026 guide says $500k.
10. **US SME risk weight in reproposal.** Simpson Thacher: no SME preferential weight; a community-bank blog says SMEs get better treatment.
