# 01. The business idea

## In one line
A **secure AI credit risk platform that each bank runs on its own machines** ("on-prem"). It grades borrowers, enforces the regulator's and the bank's rules, watches the loan book, and answers risk and compliance questions with sources.

## Problem statement: risk and compliance at banks (agreed 11–13 Nov 2026)
Banks make credit decisions on scattered, hard-to-verify information. Staff cannot quickly find reliable answers they can cite to risk and compliance questions (which rule applies, is it current, where is the official source). Credit assessment is slow and inconsistent across **individuals, businesses (SME) and corporates**. The result is delays, different people reading rules differently, repeated escalations to risk and compliance teams, operational losses, defaults and regulatory fines.

## What we build
A secure, AI-powered **Credit Risk Assessment Platform** that runs inside each bank's private systems, so customer data never leaves the bank. It:
1. **Grades risk 1–10** for individuals, businesses and corporates. It combines the bank's own data with regularly updated outside data (credit bureau, business credit, market data, sanctions lists, adverse media), fetched only through secure, approved connections (APIs).
2. **Enforces rules in three layers:** central bank → bank policy → credit policy. **Policy always overrides the AI model.** Higher-risk cases go to a human.
3. **Answers questions in plain English** in seconds, with citations to current sources, and flags uncertainty and when an expert is needed.

## How much the AI does (indicative ranges, not measured)
- **Retail** (personal loans, cards, mortgages): AI handles about **80–90%** of routine work.
- **SME and corporate:** about **60–70%**.
- **Large international banks** (for example HSBC, Citi): up to ~90%. **Regional and Islamic banks:** ~70–75% (more manual and Sharia review).
- **Human experts always handle the final 10–30%:** judgement calls, exceptions, large exposures and sign-off.

## Two editions
- **UAE Islamic edition**: Islamic finance products (murabaha, ijara and similar), Central Bank of the UAE (CBUAE) rules, Sharia review.
- **Global edition (US and UK)**: conventional banks, Fed/OCC/CFPB rules (US) and PRA/FCA/BoE rules (UK).

## Positioning: "competitive but safe"
- Easy to qualify, mid-market pricing, and **never looser than the regulator**.
- **Consulting angle** (from the mentor): show each bank where it is **"too rigid"** (missing good customers) or **"taking too much risk"**, compared with its own regulator and ~4 peer banks. Then go deeper layer by layer ("peel the onion"): risk appetite, bad-loan targets (for example 1% vs 2% non-performing loans).

## Public vs private information (why the bank must give us its policies)
Only about **20%** of what a credit decision needs is public:
- regulator rules: ~100% public;
- bank policy: ~10–20%;
- credit policy: ~20–30%.

By area:
- retail: ~35–40% public;
- SME: ~15–20%;
- corporate, compliance and collections: ~5–10%.

**Our offer:** we bring the public 20% already organised and checked (the master rulebook), and the platform holds the bank's private 80% safely inside the bank. The bank's own policies are **pasted in by the user later** through an intake template.

## Success looks like
Faster, more consistent and compliant decisions; fewer avoidable mistakes; less routine work for risk and compliance teams; lower costs, fewer fines and losses, fewer defaults and better recoveries.
