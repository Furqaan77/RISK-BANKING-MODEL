## Faisal's direction (mentor call; transcript is local only, not in the public repo)
1. Collect **central-bank rules** for **US (Fed/OCC/CFPB), UK (PRA/FCA/BoE) and UAE (CBUAE)** only. Cover retail, SME and corporate credit, plus **finance houses** (gold and cash finance). Stay inside central-bank rules; going wider confuses.
2. The central bank sets the hard limits (the "master book"). Banks operate inside them. **Compare ~4 real banks per market** against their own central bank.
3. Pitch as a consultant: "you're **too rigid** (missing opportunities)" or "you're **taking too much risk**". Peel the onion layer by layer.
4. Later: **risk appetite and bad loans** (for example 1% vs 2% NPL on a 10bn book: what it costs and what it earns).
5. Then update Tanveer sahab. Then legal, compliance and risk experts, then a business expert.
