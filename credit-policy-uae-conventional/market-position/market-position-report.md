# Where Our Credit Policy Stands in the UAE Bank Market

UAE conventional bank, retail credit policy v0.1. Market checked 9 November 2026. PDF version: *Where Our Credit Policy Stands - UAE Bank Market.pdf*.

## The short answer

Our credit policy is one of the easiest in the UAE to qualify for. It is **easier than all 16 conventional banks** with enough published data, and **5th of 22 banks** on ease of access, behind only 4 Islamic banks. Its prices sit in the **middle of the market**, as intended. Overall it ranks **9th of 22**, in the upper half of the market.

## How our policy stands out

1. **We say “yes” where others say “no”, safely**
   - **No salary transfer:** most top banks want AED 8,000–10,000. We accept **AED 5,000** with AECB ≥ 600, a listed employer and 6 months in the job.
   - **Car loans:** most top banks want AED 7,000. We accept **AED 5,000**.
   - **First home, UAE nationals:** Mashreq and RAKBANK want AED 15,000. We accept **AED 10,000**, after a stress test.
2. **Products the big conventional banks don’t offer**
   - **Salary advance** before payday.
   - **Finance secured by end-of-service benefits**, priced near the floor.
   - **Lending against gold**, plus a clear published rule for card limits.
3. **Fair, transparent pricing**
   - Rates near the market middle, **priced by risk grade**, so good customers pay less.
   - **No processing fee** on buyouts.
   - Home loans: **25% free prepayment** a year, lower valuation fee, processing capped at AED 50,000.
4. **Speed**
   - The AI Risk Score Card decides in **about 3 seconds**.
   - Buyouts paid to the other bank **within 30 days**.
   - UAE nationals get up to **120 days** before the first instalment.
5. **Safety built in**
   - **Credit-score floors** and automatic declines for serious arrears.
   - Home loans **stress-tested** at a higher rate.
   - **Nightly early warning** on every customer; rate +2% if salary stops; portfolio limits.
6. **Fair to everyone**
   - **No nationality bans.**
   - Employers are **grouped by risk, not excluded**.
   - Every decision comes with clear reasons the customer can understand.

## Real customers, real answers

### Scenario 1: A nurse whose salary goes to another bank
Anita, 29, expat nurse at a listed private hospital (category C). Salary AED 6,500, paid to another bank. AECB 655, 14 months in the job, one card with an AED 10,000 limit.

- FAB (no salary transfer): Needs AED 10,000 → **no**
- Emirates NBD (no salary transfer): Needs AED 10,000 → **no**
- RAKBANK (no salary transfer): Yes: AECB ≥ 541; 10× salary at 12.99–22.49%
- Mashreq NEO: Yes: up to AED 150,000 at 10.49–17.49%
- **Our policy**: **Yes.** AECB 655 ≥ 600; listed employer; 14 months > 6

- **Limit:** lower of 10× salary and the AED 200,000 cap = **AED 65,000**.
- **Room each month:** 50% of AED 6,500 = 3,250, minus AED 500 for the card = **AED 2,750**.
- **Instalment:** grade 4 (11.99%), 48 months ≈ **AED 1,711**, inside the room.

### Scenario 2: Moving a loan to us (buyout), UAE national
Khalid, 34, UAE national, Abu Dhabi government (category A). Salary AED 32,000. Existing loan: AED 400,000 left at 8.49%, 36 months to go.

- Other bank today: 8.49% on AED 400,000
- **Our policy**: **5.49%** (grades 1–3), paid to the other bank within 30 days, **no processing fee**

- Instalment falls from about **AED 12,625** to **AED 12,077**: **AED 549 a month**, about **AED 19,750** over 36 months.
- After the other bank's 1% early-settlement charge (AED 4,000): **net ≈ AED 15,750**.
- **Top-up:** +AED 100,000 → AED 500,000 (limit 20× = 640,000); over 48 months ≈ AED 11,626 = **36% of salary**, under 50%.

### Scenario 3: First car for a lower-paid worker
Joseph, 31, expat supervisor at a logistics company. Salary AED 5,500, transferred to us. Wants a 4-year-old used car for AED 60,000.

- Emirates NBD, FAB, Mashreq: Need AED 7,000 → **no**
- ADCB, RAKBANK: AED 5,000 → yes
- **Our policy**: **Yes**, minimum AED 5,000

- **Loan:** AED 12,000 down (20%), **AED 48,000** at 3.19% flat over 60 months.
- **Instalment:** interest AED 7,656 → about **AED 928 a month = 17% of salary**.
- **Car age:** 4 + 5 = 9 years at the end, inside the 10-year limit.

### Scenario 4: First home for a young Emirati
Mariam, 28, UAE national, semi-government employer (category A). Salary AED 12,000. First home in Sharjah, AED 900,000.

- Mashreq, RAKBANK: Need AED 15,000 → **no**
- Emirates NBD (10,000), ADCB (8,000): Yes
- **Our policy**: **Yes**, AED 10,000 minimum for nationals

- **Loan:** 85% of value = **AED 765,000** over 25 years.

- **Stress test** at 3M EIBOR 4.39% + 1.79% = 6.18%: about **AED 5,013 = 42%** of salary. She passes.

### Scenario 5: End-of-service benefits for a better rate
Suresh, 45, expat engineer, listed large company (category B), 9 years' service. Basic AED 14,000, gross 22,000. Needs AED 100,000 for university fees.

- Large UAE banks: Take end-of-service benefits only as **extra** security; none lends against them
- **Our policy**: **Yes**, with the employer's undertaking

- **Benefit:** 21 days × 5 years + 30 days × 4 years = 225 days ≈ **AED 103,562**; 80% ≈ **AED 82,849** = 83% of the loan.
- **Rate:** **5.99%** instead of 9.99%.
- **Saving:** AED 2,348 a month instead of 2,536 ≈ **AED 9,000** over 48 months.

### Scenario 6: Help before payday
Ramesh, 40, construction foreman. Net salary AED 5,200, transferred to us. Needs AED 2,500 for school fees 10 days before payday.

- Five largest conventional banks: No published salary advance
- DIB, ADIB (Islamic): Offer one
- **Our policy**: **Yes**: up to 50% of net salary

- **Maximum:** 50% of AED 5,200 = **AED 2,600**.
- **Approved:** AED 2,500 for a fixed fee of **AED 100**.
- **Repaid** automatically from the next salary.

### Scenario 7: Borrowing against gold
Fatima, 50, holds gold certificates for 200 g, worth about AED 90,000 at an illustrative AED 450 per gram.

- Conventional UAE banks: None offers it
- Emirates Islamic: 50%, Priority customers only
- **Our policy**: **70%** of gold value

- **Advance:** 70% ≈ **AED 63,000**.
- **Top-up call** if gold falls **12.5%** (value AED 78,750).
- **Sale after notice** if it falls **17.6%** (value about AED 74,160).

### Scenario 8: Where we say “no”, and why that is good
Same profile as Anita, but AECB 560 and a non-listed employer (category D).

- No salary transfer: Needs AED 10,000 and AECB 600 → **declined**
- **Counter-offer**: Move the salary to us: from AED 5,000 with AECB ≥ 541, the salary-assigned loan is open

- The customer is **not just turned away**; she is offered a safer route.
- **Four months later**, if her salary stops arriving, the **nightly check** flags it.
- The rate steps up 2% and collections call her **within 5 working days**, before a missed payment becomes a default.
