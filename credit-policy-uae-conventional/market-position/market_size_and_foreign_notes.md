## Foreign-bank notes
- **Citi UAE:** retail NOT sold. Card KFS updated Jul-2026; no standalone personal loan; Ready Credit up to 6× salary from 15%.
- **Most foreign branches barely lend to retail customers.** No unsecured personal loans at HBL, ABK, NBK, Doha Bank, Banque Misr, Janata, QNB, Banque Banorient France or Al Khaliji France. HBL, Janata, Banorient and Al Khaliji publish no retail lending at all; Doha Bank shows only legacy fees.
- **HSBC:** personal loan only with salary transfer (7,500 approved employers / 12,500 others); card 5,000 / 12,500; home 15k, LTV 80/75; 3M EIBOR + 0.99% variable.
- **Standard Chartered:** home 30k conventional (15k Saadiq), LTV 80/75; card 8,000; credit card + personal loan book only AED 0.37bn; no live personal-loan page.
- **HBZ:** cards for self-employed (AED 60k business income) at 1.99% per month; overdraft 100% against time deposit; auto from 3.50% flat.
- **NBK:** home loans from about AED 18.4k salary, LTV 65%.
- **Bank Melli:** barred by the CBUAE from Iran transactions (Sep-2026). **Janata:** UAE branches face closure.
- **No onshore retail lending:** MCB, SBI, ICICI (SBI and ICICI are DIFC-only).
