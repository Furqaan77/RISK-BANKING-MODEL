# Conventional Bank Retail Credit Policy: Parameter Sheet (single source of truth)

**Basis:** the credit criteria recommended for the Islamic policy (`../credit-policy-uae/03-parameter-sheet.md`), kept unchanged as requested, converted to conventional structures, and re-benchmarked against the **top five conventional UAE retail lenders**: Emirates NBD (conventional), FAB, ADCB, Mashreq, RAKBANK. Benchmark data: `01-benchmark-conventional.md` (checked 2026-11-09).

**Ratings** (policy criterion vs the five conventional banks):
- **Near:** within their range, at or close to the median.
- **Lenient edge:** inside the range but at the customer-friendly end; tighten if the risk appetite is lower.
- **No peer product:** none of the five publishes this product or figure; ours is a proposal or a differentiator.
- **n/a:** out of scope.

**Source tags:**
- [C] CBUAE rule
- [M] conventional top-five median
- [I] carried over from the recommended Islamic policy
- [P] proposal (no market figure)
- [CV] converted to conventional practice

Rates are reducing-balance unless marked flat. Fees exclude 5% VAT unless stated.

**Overall:** on the conventional benchmark the recommended criteria sit **within the top-five range on every published item**. Four items are at the customer-friendly end; tighten those (auto 7k, home nationals 12.5–15k, expat home service 12 months, SE auto balance 20k) for a more conservative appetite. Six items have no published conventional peer: they are proposals or differentiators and need product approval. Nothing is looser than the CBUAE.
