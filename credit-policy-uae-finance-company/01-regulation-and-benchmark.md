# UAE Finance Companies: Regulation and Retail-Lending Benchmark (status 9 Nov 2026)

**Confidence:**
- **H:** read directly (CB Register Aug-2026, FSR 2025, BNPL press release 27-Dec-2023, Consumer Protection Standards, company KFSs).
- **M:** rulebook search extracts (rulebook.centralbank.ae returned 403).
- **S / L:** secondary sources.

## Five points that matter most
1. **Governing regulation:** Finance Companies Regulation (FCR), Circular C 3/2023 as amended (rulebook effective 29 Sep 2023; press release 27 Dec 2023).
2. **Consumer rules apply as for banks:** Regulation 29/2011 (20× salary, 48 months, DBR 50%, fee caps), Consumer Protection Regulation and Standards, Credit Risk Management Regulation C 3/2024.
3. **Leverage is the binding growth limit:** total liabilities ≤ **100% of capital funds in years 0–2**.
4. **Minimum-salary removal (Nov 2025) is press-reported only;** applicability to finance companies is not explicit.
5. **The sector is small, shrinking and turning digital:** 20 licensed finance companies, about AED 11.2bn of loans; several legacy players are in run-off.

## E. FC vs bank lending
| Dimension | Finance companies | Banks |
|---|---|---|
| Segments | non-STL salaried, low-income / WPS, thin-file digital, nationals buyout niche, SME / self-employed, captive auto | STL salaried, listed employers, AED 5k+ |
| Personal-loan pricing | **14–40% reducing**; digital APR 16–54%+ | ~5–20% (STL); ~9–22% (non-STL) |
| Risk | net NPL **7.1%**; Finance House ECL ~21% | NPL 3.3%, net 1.6% |
| Funding | no retail deposits; corporate deposits, bank lines, securitisation | retail deposits |
| Capital | leverage ≤ 1× (yrs 0–2) up to 7×; single borrower ≤ 10% | Basel III; large exposure ≤ 25% Tier 1 |
| Conduct rules | **same as banks** | same |
| Trend | small, shrinking; legacy exiting; new digital entrants | growing retail (+16% 2025) |
