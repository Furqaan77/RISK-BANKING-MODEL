# Pilot Finance Company Retail Credit Policy: Parameter Sheet (single source of truth)

**Basis:** the credit criteria recommended for the UAE bank policies (`../credit-policy-uae-conventional/03-parameter-sheet.md`), kept where they fit and adjusted to the Finance Companies Regulation (FCR) and the finance-company market. Market data: `01-regulation-and-benchmark.md` (checked 2026-11-09). Assumptions: `00-decisions.md`.

**Compared with:**
- **UAE finance companies:** Finance House, Deem, Al Ain Finance, Aafaq, Mawarid, Siraj, Al Futtaim Finance, botim / CashNow, e& easyCredit; BNPL: Tamara, Tabby.
- **Top five conventional banks:** Emirates NBD, FAB, ADCB, Mashreq, RAKBANK.

**Ratings** (policy item vs the finance-company market):
- **Near:** inside the finance-company range, at or close to the typical value.
- **Tighter:** stricter criterion or higher price than the finance-company typical (deliberate).
- **Competitive edge:** better for the customer than the finance-company typical, still inside the market range.
- **No FC peer:** no finance company publishes this product or figure; ours is a proposal.
- **Required (FCR):** required by the Finance Companies Regulation; not a market choice.
- **Not offered:** not offered in the pilot (not permitted, or deferred to phase 2).

**Source tags:**
- [B] bank criterion carried over unchanged
- [C] CBUAE consumer rule (Regulation 29/2011, Consumer Protection Standards)
- [R] Finance Companies Regulation rule
- [A] adjusted for the finance-company rules or market (needs sign-off)
- [P] proposal (no published market figure; needs sign-off)

Rates are reducing-balance unless marked flat. Fees exclude 5% VAT unless stated. FC = finance company.

**Overall:** the policy sits inside the finance-company range on 38 of 60 items and is better for customers on 3 (personal-loan price, card rate, gold price). It is deliberately tighter on 4 (AECB floor, auto price, gold LTV, BNPL caps). 8 items are finance-company legal requirements, 5 have no finance-company peer and 2 products are not offered in the pilot. Nothing is looser than the CBUAE or the FCR.
